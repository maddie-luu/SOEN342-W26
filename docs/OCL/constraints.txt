“A task cannot have more than 20 sub-tasks.”. 

“The number of open tasks without a due date should not exceed 50.” 

“The limit for open tasks for each collaborator category is a positive integer.” (i.e. Senior collaborators are limited to 2 open tasks.) 

context Collaborator
inv openTaskLimitPositive:
    self.getOpenTaskLimit() > 0

inv defaultLimitsByCategory:
    (self.category.toLower() = 'senior' implies self.getOpenTaskLimit() = 2) and
    (self.category.toLower() = 'intermediate' implies self.getOpenTaskLimit() = 5) and
    (self.category.toLower() = 'junior' implies self.getOpenTaskLimit() = 10)


“No collaborator must be overloaded”. (the number of assigned tasks that are open should not exceed the limit.)
